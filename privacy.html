Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Botfolio publishes educational short videos about building AI bots without code. "Botfolio Engine" is our internal tool that publishes our own original content to our own social media accounts (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, X).

What data we access

Botfolio Engine only connects to accounts owned and operated by Botfolio. Through the official platform APIs it uses basic profile information of our own account (user ID, username) and permission to upload and publish videos and images to our own account. We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other users, viewers or followers.

How we use it

Only to publish our own content and to check whether a post was published successfully. Access tokens are stored locally on our own computer and are never shared with third parties.

Retention and deletion

We keep access tokens only while the integration is active. You can ask us to delete any data related to you at any time by email; we respond within 30 days.

AI-generated content

Our hosts (Sun, Luna and Ray) are AI-generated avatars. Every post is labelled as AI-generated.
